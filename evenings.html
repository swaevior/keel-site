Already built

A machine with five stations

Every day, market prices flow through the same steps. The fourth one can say no to anything.

Watch

Collects prices and checks they're fresh and sane.

Forecast

Scores each market from −20 to +20: how strong is the trend?

Size

Turns the score into a bet size, smaller when markets get jumpy.

Safety check

Separate rules that can veto or shrink any order. It never sees the forecast.

Trade

Places the orders. Practice money only, for now.
